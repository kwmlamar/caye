## What moved, and what did not

The gain is concentrated in dimensions that were previously unmeasurable rather than
absent. `workspace_context_isolation` now passes outright, `continuous_learning` and
`observability` became evaluable, and the `coverage` and `isolation` subsystem failures
cleared entirely — all consequences of the isolated production adapter replacing
hand-authored unevaluable traces.

Every dimension requiring Caye to *initiate* is unchanged at or near zero:
`proactive_opportunity_detection`, `task_completion`, `temporal_reasoning`,
`memory_correctness` and `contradiction_handling`. The adapter deliberately does not
synthesize opportunity or action state, so these are measurements of real capability,
not of missing instrumentation.

Both scenario economic ledgers remain zero. This is intentional evidence: the system
still cannot demonstrate the benchmarked workload reduction or revenue protection
through an evaluable end-to-end replay.
