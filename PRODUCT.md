## Platform

web

## Users

Owner-operated Caribbean small businesses. Proven wedge: tour/excursion operators, charter companies, and boat/equipment rentals in the Bahamas. ICP has since widened to any WhatsApp-heavy small business with a stable service/price catalog, targeting globally including the US (Caribbean stays the marketing/brand identity, not the targeting constraint). The buyer and day-to-day user is whoever personally handles customer messages — often not the titular owner (e.g. the owner's spouse) — and is overwhelmed answering bookings across WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, and email around a day job.

## Product Purpose

Caye is a WhatsApp-first AI hire, not a SaaS app. She answers customer messages in the owner's own voice, checks the calendar, sends the booking link, follows up on leads, and — in a back-office mode — runs decisions and ops visibility for the owner and staff. Success means no customer message ever falls through the cracks, and the owner gets back the hours they used to spend on manual replies.

## Positioning

Not a chatbot, automation tool, or CRM — "a hire." The distinguishing mechanism: she lives inside tools the owner already uses (their own WhatsApp number, their own Zoho/Google inbox and calendar) instead of requiring a new app or dashboard, and she's built via discovery-first onboarding (learns voice and prices from the owner's own past messages) rather than a manual setup wizard. Front desk mode never reveals she's AI to guests; back-office mode is transparently AI to the owner/staff.

## Operating Context

Currently one live paid workflow end-to-end: Bimini Island Tours (Zoho Mail + WhatsApp front desk, back-office ops on WhatsApp). Booking confirmation today sends a payment link (WeTravel), not a native in-chat button — WhatsApp's API doesn't support that UI. Gmail/Google Workspace integration is designed but not yet live (Zoho is the only proven email channel); Instagram/Messenger webhooks are built but not proven end-to-end. No pricing page or multi-page site exists — this is a single long-scroll landing page, sales-led (primary CTA goes to a WhatsApp conversation / demo, not self-serve signup).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- $79/mo flat pricing through the first 10 paying customers; re-prices to a tiered structure after that.
- Exactly one paying customer as of now (Bimini Island Tours, live since July 2026) — no logo wall, no fabricated testimonials or usage stats. The page's testimonial slot is built but intentionally gated off until there's a real quote.
- Unpaid pilot / founding-discount offers are closed; all new customers pay from day one. Copy implying an ongoing free trial is stale, not current.
- "Hire," not "AI employee" or "AI staff," is the locked descriptor for Caye herself (2026-07-28 decision) — job-ownership phrasing ("she runs your front desk") over a generic AI-tool label, to avoid the genericized "AI employee" category competitors already occupy.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Caye (pronounced "key," named for the sandbars off the islands). Company: TropiTech Solutions, a Bahamian company (founder: Lamar).
- Visual identity: warm cream background, near-black ink, Caribbean teal/coral/gold accents; editorial-leaning display type (hero headline currently Geist at black weight, Newsreader for body/subhead, mono for the eyebrow) — this system is mid-iteration as of this session, expect further changes.
- Marketing voice stays Caribbean/Bahamian even though outreach targeting has widened globally.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real, verifiable facts only: Bimini Island Tours is the one live paying customer; her shipped capabilities (WhatsApp/email replies, calendar check, booking-link send, back-office digest and decisions queue) are real and live.
- No verbatim customer testimonial exists yet — do not fabricate one. No case studies, press mentions, or a second customer logo exist yet.

## Product Principles

- Compete against hiring a person, not against other software — every surface should make "hire her" feel like the natural alternative to hiring a human, not a tool comparison.
- Never claim a capability that isn't actually live (native in-chat booking, Gmail, proven IG/Messenger, usage stats, testimonials) — accuracy discipline outranks persuasive polish.
- WhatsApp is where the owner talks to Caye, not the product itself — she works across channels and the back office too.
- Premium, human, confident — no AI hype, no corporate jargon, no exaggerated claims.
