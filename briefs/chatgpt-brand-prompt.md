## What Caye actually is

Caye is a WhatsApp-first AI hire for small Caribbean tourism/service businesses — tour
operators, boat charters, small hospitality — not a SaaS app, not a chatbot widget, not a
"workforce" of bots. The current tagline, live on the site, is **"Not a tool. A hire."**
That line is a locked positioning decision, not copy up for grabs — but everything
around it (visual identity, voice, how the brand shows up) is open for a serious
rethink.

**The product, concretely:**
- A business owner texts Caye on WhatsApp like a staff member. She answers guest
  inquiries (email, WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger), handles bookings, knows the
  business's policies and pricing, and escalates to the owner only when something
  genuinely needs a human call.
- There is deliberately **no dashboard app** for the day-to-day. The dashboard is locked
  to signup, billing, and channel-connection handoff only — everything else happens by
  talking to her. This is the single sharpest wedge against every competitor: "you don't
  learn a new tool, you just text her."
- Positioning has already moved once — from "WhatsApp-first coverage" to **"a management
  surface, not coverage"** — the pitch isn't "she's always online," it's "you run your
  business by texting her like a member of staff."
- Target customer: a solo or small-team Caribbean tourism operator — not technical, often
  running the whole business off a personal phone, skeptical of software, trusts
  relationships over platforms. Real example: a Bimini, Bahamas island-tour operator.
- Voice, already locked: **Bahamian directness.** No soft American hedge language ("no
  rush," "just floating this back up"). Direct, warm, blunt when it needs to be — not
  corporate-polite.
- Known competitor: Cerebra Caribbean — a broader, multi-vertical Caribbean AI
  chatbot/voice platform that publishes pricing and testimonials, positioned as a
  product/platform rather than a hire.
- Pricing: $79/mo flat, single tier, pre-revenue-stage company with currently one paying
  customer.

## What "A++ Caye" means — the aspirational end state

If Caye is executed as well as she possibly can be, she becomes an AI employee that:

1. **Remembers everything** — every policy, every quirk of the business, taught once,
   never re-explained.
2. **Knows what's actually happening in the business right now** — not a chatbot that
   answers when spoken to, an employee who's aware, the way a real staff member would be.
3. **Helps in every way she plausibly can** — not scoped to "answering messages," scoped
   to "running the parts of the business that can be run by someone trustworthy who
   never sleeps."
4. **Proactively makes the business better** — surfaces the thing the owner didn't know
   to ask about, not just answers to what they did ask.

In one sentence: **the best employee you've ever hired, who happens to live in your
WhatsApp.** Not a feature set. Not "AI-powered." A relationship with someone who works
for you.
